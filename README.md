# Moxa Sourcing Guide

A buyer-side checklist for evaluating moxa suppliers, plus a short explainer on moxa wool ratios. Written to apply to any supplier — including ourselves.

## 10-Point Supplier Checklist

Items 1–4 are disqualifying if unanswered; items 5–10 decide who you stay with.

1. **Harvest year in writing.** Can the supplier state the harvest year and aging period of the material in your order, in writing?
2. **Ratio mass balance.** For a high-ratio wool (e.g. 30:1), can they explain input weight, output weight and where the remainder went?
3. **Retention sample per batch.** Is a retained sample kept for each batch, so quality can be traced later?
4. **Batch records and test reports.** Is there a batch record with ratio verification and test documentation, rather than a generic certificate?
5. **MOQ structure.** Is there a trial tier for new partners?
6. **Packaging flexibility.** Can packaging follow private-label requirements?
7. **Compliance support.** Can labeling and export documents be prepared for your target market?
8. **Lead time communication.** Are sample, production and shipping timelines stated clearly?
9. **Traceability.** Is the material origin identifiable per batch?
10. **Consistency.** Does repeat production match the approved sample?

## Understanding Moxa Wool Ratios

- Ratios such as 5:1, 10:1, 15:1, 20:1 and 30:1 describe how much raw leaf goes into a given amount of finished wool.
- Higher ratios generally produce finer, lighter wool; the ratio claim should be verifiable by material balance, not just labeling.
- Aging and storage conditions also affect the final material.
